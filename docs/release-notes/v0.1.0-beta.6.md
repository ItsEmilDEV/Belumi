# Belumi 0.1.0 beta 6

A prerelease: try it, tell us what breaks. Everything new is described in [docs/client-features.md](https://github.com/ItsEmilDEV/Belumi/blob/main/docs/client-features.md).

## New in the game
- **Distant chunks** (like Bobby): chunks a server forgot stay drawn out to your render distance and are cached for the next visit.
- **Changes since your last visit**, **chest memory** (`/chests`), **way back** (compass, breadcrumb trail, portal and spawn waypoints).
- **Death clips** with a killer camera, **replay video** (F7, needs ffmpeg), **photo mode** (orbit, time-lapse, 360° panorama).
- **Combat log**, **world history** with `/rollback`, **redstone workbench** (signal numbers, `/tick` buttons, profiler).
- **Blueprint** (`.schem`, `.litematic`) with rotate and mirror, **pings and shared waypoints** between Belumi players.
- **Plugins** can have their own screens; the launcher installs plugins from a list.
- From well-known mods: **Accurate Block Placement**, **Light Overlay**, **Jade-style Inspect** module, **Gamma boost**, **Sound controller**, **ender chest tooltip**, **Riptide fix**.
- **Account switch** in the game, **join links** (`belumi://join/host:port`).

## Good to know
- Accurate placement, gamma and the Riptide fix are off by default: some servers count them as help.
- Pings and shared waypoints only work on Horizon servers (dedicated, LAN, single player).
- Sound physics (reverb) is off by default and was checked by tests, not by ear.
