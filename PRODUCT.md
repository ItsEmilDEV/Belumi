# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Players of Minecraft: Java Edition on Linux and Windows desktops who want a faster, lighter client. They open the launcher to start a game within seconds, switch between a few accounts, pick a server and sometimes read the log when something breaks. Mostly evenings, dark rooms, a 16:9 monitor, mouse and keyboard.

## Product Purpose

The Belumi launcher signs in Microsoft accounts (device code), keeps several accounts, renews them, lists servers with their game version and starts the Belumi client (a Rust reimplementation of the game). Success: from opening the launcher to a running game in under ten seconds, and every failure explained in one sentence.

## Brand Personality

Quick, honest, a little playful. Calm by default; the Minecraft style leans into the blocky game heritage without borrowing any of Mojang's art.

## Anti-references

Web-landing-page gloss, neon gamer dashboards, stock launcher clutter (news feeds, ads, sliders everywhere), purple-gradient hero blocks, cards that nest cards.

## Design Principles

1. The Play action is always one obvious click away.
2. Say what is happening: account state, renewal, errors, in plain words.
3. One accent, used for the primary action and the active state only.
4. Three looks, one layout: Modern, Modern Glass and Minecraft change materials, not structure.
5. Nothing animates that the player did not cause.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast 4.5:1 or better in every style; every control reachable by keyboard with a visible focus ring; no information by colour alone (state dots carry words).

## Assumptions

Written without an interview, from the project brief and the existing launcher: the user asked for autonomous work. Revisit when the product owner has opinions.
